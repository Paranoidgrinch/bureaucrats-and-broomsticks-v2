## Play the beta

### 1. Download

Click the button above or download:

**[Bureaucrats-and-Broomsticks-Windows-x64.zip](https://github.com/Paranoidgrinch/bureaucrats-and-broomsticks-v2/releases/download/v0.1.0-beta.1/Bureaucrats-and-Broomsticks-Windows-x64.zip)**

### 2. Extract everything

Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose **Extract All**.

Do not run the game from inside the ZIP.

### 3. Start the game

Open the extracted folder and double-click:

```text
Bureaucrats-and-Broomsticks.exe
```

Keep the EXE and the included `_internal` folder together.

**No installation and no Python setup are required.**

> Windows may display a warning because this independent beta build is not digitally signed. Choose **More info → Run anyway** to start it.

## Welcome to the administration of magic

Magic has not disappeared.

It has been **licensed, archived, audited and assigned to the appropriate department**.

Choose one of nine unusual professions and fight through five acts of increasingly hostile bureaucracy:

**The City → The Archives → The Green Docket → The Licensing Labyrinth → The Divine Ledger**

Build your deck, collect relics, combine status effects and survive enemies ranging from street-level administrative problems to divine accounting.

## How to play

All available choices are displayed directly in the terminal.

* Enter a displayed **number** to choose a class, route, card, target or reward.
* Type `end` to finish your combat turn.
* Type `log` to view the complete combat log.
* Type `quit` to leave the game.

Enemy intents, health, block, energy, card costs and active statuses are shown during combat.

## What comes next?

*Bureaucrats and Broomsticks* became a large experiment in deterministic combat, data-driven content and automated playtesting.

Its architectural limits led directly to:

### ⚙️ [RogueDeck Core](https://github.com/Paranoidgrinch/RogueDeck-Core)

A modular C# roguelike deckbuilding engine designed so that new mechanics extend the system instead of becoming another hardcoded exception.
